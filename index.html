Streaming Safety & Adblock Guide

extension
1. Install uBlock Origin Never browse free streaming sites without an authentic adblocker. uBlock Origin neutralizes hidden popunders and rogue redirect scripts.
public
2. Encrypted DNS (Bypass ISP Blocks) If a site doesn't load, your ISP is likely blocking the domain. Enable DNS over HTTPS (DoH) via Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) or NextDNS inside your browser settings.
shield
3. Brave Browser / Firefox Chrome's Manifest V3 limits adblocking extensions. Firefox and Brave provide the strongest protection against deceptive malware players.
