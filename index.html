Basic Streaming Checklist (Do this once)
1. uBlock Origin

Mandatory extension. Blocks popup tabs, redirect loops, and hidden video overlays.

2. Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1)

If any site fails to load, your ISP is blocking it. Set device DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 to unblock.

3. Brave or Firefox

Chromium browsers restrict adblock extensions. Firefox and Brave provide unrestricted filtering.
